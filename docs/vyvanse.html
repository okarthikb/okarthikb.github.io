initial experience with Vyvanse

physical relaxation somewhat noticeable and mind being quieter and improved mood are more noticeable. this has been very weird, because i used to have intrusive rumination that physically agitated me – think clenching teeth and muscle in the middle of coding or reading, or repeated stimming on back of my head upon negative or positive arousal

get up less often for random things, won't get distracted by the carpet alignment in the middle of work. uh, i suppose i should've noticed this sort of thing earlier

can drown out background noise better now. used to lose focus or get very minor anxiety spikes from background noise, like clock sounds, elevator noise, notifications, calls, etc. i don't end up spiraling into thinking about all sorts of shit because a uni email or tweet triggered a branch. app/site blockers help

the distractions still persist but at low 20 mg dose (always ramped up later for most people), but i've had more instances of telling my brain to stfu

first instinct is to yell "this is just placebo" because i never even considered i might have ADHD before and this is just regular anxiety everyone faces, but i'm guessing it's not

benefits for a while delayed by heavy sleep disruption because i took it afternoon for one day like an idiot, slept late, got sick, bad loop for a couple of days. fixed with some melatonin. fixing sleep right now so it's not a confounder – Fernando's blogpost advice is pretty good. sleep is by far most important

noticeably lower appetite, which is a nice-to-have. when i get a craving i go to the kitchen or bathroom with my phone and end up wasting an hour because i got distracted and forgot where i left off

realizing much anxiety is downstream of ADHD. can't imagine how i got anything done consistently
